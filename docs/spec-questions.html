What the spec proposes (2-minute read)

The assessor loads a site plan or a map, sets its scale with two clicks and a known distance, marks the asset and the line of protection, then draws approach routes. The tool turns each drawn route into the straights and curves the current calculator already uses. The maths is unchanged and every result can still be checked by hand.

  • Corners are rounded automatically. Each clicked vertex becomes an arc; tangent length = r·tan(θ/2), arc length = r·θ.
  • The fastest line, not the centreline. Drivers cut corners. For a bend of deflection θ, traversable width w and inner kerb radius rᵢ:
R = rᵢ + w / (1 − cos(θ/2))

On a 7 m road at a sharp 90° junction the centreline gives a car about 23 km/h. The corner-cutting line gives about 47 km/h.

  • New results at the line of protection: impact angle, impact energy, the bend that limits speed, and the required rating (e.g. V/7200[N2]/48/90).
  • Mitigation: barrier lines with a gap check, gates and forced stops, and as-is vs option comparisons.
  • Phases: P0 foundations · P1 offline plan upload (the core ask) · P2 live maps · P3 automation · P4 auto-generated routes.
