    Vectors

    Searches for the fastest approaches to each line of protection. "Follow roads" keeps to the mapped roads, service roads and paths, and leaves them only for the last stretch to the line. "Roads and open ground" also tries straight runs across open land and round building corners. Buildings, walls, lakes, ponds and rivers block every route. Results are suggestions: accept or reject each one.

      Required protection

      Assumptions and limitations
      • Speeds come from the same engine as the route calculator: fitted acceleration curves per vehicle class (top speed reached after 440 m), cornering at v = √(μ·r·g) capped at each class's slip speed, on a flat, level, dry surface with a perfect driver.
      • Braking into a bend is instant and the bend speed is held throughout; vehicles never exceed their class's top speed.
      • Everything that is not an obstacle counts as drivable at road grip, including grass, verges and kerbs, which overstates speed there.
      • Bends use the largest radius that fits and keeps clear of obstacles (a driver cutting corners). Found vectors keep 1 m clear of obstacles; drawn vectors are taken as drawn.
      • Found vectors: straight runs, runs round building corners and routes along the road network up to the study radius. Obstacles are buildings and mapped walls and vehicle barriers from OpenStreetMap, plus any you draw; fences, hedges, gates and bollards are not counted. A run that starts at an obstacle starts from rest. They are suggestions and need checking: missing or extra buildings in OpenStreetMap change the results.
      • The speed engine has been checked against NPSA HOVVA: all 156 test values (13 routes, standing and rolling starts, six vehicle classes) were identical.
      • Impact energy uses nominal class masses (1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 7.5, 18 and 30 t).
      • Results support, but do not replace, a competent specialist assessment.
